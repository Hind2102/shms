How hazards are processed

Every report follows the same path. Each hazard page shows where it is now, who did each step and when (from the audit trail), and who acts next.

Who acts at each stage

StageWho acts next
ReportedThe reporter confirms the hazard type and consequence (the AI only suggests).
Confirmed & risk-scoredAutomatic: risk = consequence × likelihood; routed to the Area Owner. High/Extreme or consequence ≥ 4 alert the safety supervisor.
Assigned to Area OwnerArea Owner plans the action (action owner and deadline), or closes it if already fixed.
Action plannedThe action owner carries out the action; the Area Owner closes it with the control used and the residual risk.
Closed, awaiting verificationThe safety supervisor or the reporter verifies – never the person who closed it.
Verified closedNo action. A supervisor or the reporter can reopen it.

Assignment is automatic: confirming a report routes it to the owner of its area; there is no separate assign step.
