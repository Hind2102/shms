Risk matrix

From the ADOSH-SF guideline

The structure follows the Abu Dhabi Occupational Safety and Health System Framework (ADOSH-SF) Risk Management Technical Guideline: risk is rated as consequence × likelihood on a 5 × 5 matrix, every level has a written definition, and the required response depends on the resulting risk level.

The level wording, score bands and close-out deadlines below are this project's own choices. They have not yet been checked line by line against the official guideline text; that check is an open action for the project owner.
