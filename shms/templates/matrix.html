Defined by this project

Likelihood from the area's history – a project heuristic

A project heuristic, not a validated likelihood. The system sets the likelihood from the number of confirmed hazards of the same type in the same area in the last {{ risk.lookback_days() }} days. It does not know about exposure time, controls in place or reporting habits, and it has not been compared with incident data.

Alerts

High and Extreme hazards alert the safety supervisors and the Area Owner. A hazard with consequence {{ risk.high_consequence_min() }} or 5 also alerts them when its score is Low or Moderate, because the guideline asks for particular attention to risks with a very low likelihood but major consequences (Sec. 5(b), p. 5); the threshold of {{ risk.high_consequence_min() }} is the project's reading.

All values are in shms/config/risk_matrix.json; the source, page numbers and the PDF's SHA-256 are in docs/risk_matrix_source.md.
